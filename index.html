About Christina's Hair Salon

Christina's Hair Salon, also known as Hair Couture, has been giving Milpitas quality hair care for over 14 years. You'll find us inside Seafood City on Landess Avenue. We're a friendly, Filipino-operated salon with professional stylists who pay close attention to every detail of your next look.
